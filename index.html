Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic acid)

Clinical use: Analgesic, antipyretic, anti-inflammatory, and antiplatelet therapy.

Mechanism: Irreversible cyclooxygenase inhibition, reducing prostaglandin and thromboxane synthesis.

Toxicology note: Overdose may cause salicylate toxicity with tinnitus, acid-base disturbance, and hyperventilation.

    E-C-A: Erythromycin, Clarithromycin, Azithromycin.
